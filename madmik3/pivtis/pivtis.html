Pivtis

Move the falling piece left and right. Press space to rotate. Hold down arrow to drop faster.

When one colored block lands, the white pivot can rotate the piece.

When the piece settles, the white pivot disappears and the colored blocks fall.

Clear groups of 4 or more touching blocks of the same color.

After a clear, blocks fall. Then reach-lines can connect same-color blocks across empty horizontal space.

Blocks score 1 point each. Chains multiply fast: 1x, 5x, 10x, 20x.

The speed is based on how many blocks are on screen, and the base speed also increases every 10 drops.

Music starts after your first keypress and intensifies as the speed rises.
